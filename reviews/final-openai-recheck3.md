## Findings by severity

### P2

- **R5-01 · Merge blocker · The Booking focus retry can take focus out of a reopened mobile menu.** At 390 × 844, open the menu, focus its Booking link and press Enter. Reopen the menu from the header toggle while the 150 ms retry is pending. In Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, focus starts on Music inside the reopened menu, then moves to `#booking-title` while the sheet is still open. The next Tab goes to the obscured booking e-mail link in Chromium and Firefox or to Name in WebKit. Escape closes the sheet and restores the toggle. In additional timed runs the failure reproduced at 94–136 ms between activation clicks in Chromium and 119–138 ms in WebKit. A reopen after the retry kept focus on Music. The guard treats focus in any instance of the reused sheet as lost focus from the closed menu. Cancel both scheduled focus callbacks on a new menu session or require the sheet to still be closed when the retry runs. Add a regression check that tabs inside the reopened sheet. [Header.tsx](repo/src/components/site/Header.tsx:124) · [German preview](https://typhoon-band-website-80isw0bgx-mikamcflurrys-projects.vercel.app/de)

### P3

- **R5-02 · Existing behavior · A legal link in consent settings leaves the modal over its destination.** At 320 × 568, open **Datenschutz-Einstellungen**, scroll inside the dialog and activate **Datenschutz**. All three engines navigate to `/de/legal/privacy` with the dialog still covering the legal content. Chromium and Firefox remain inside the dialog. WebKit dropped focus to `BODY`, where seven Tab presses left it. Escape dismissed the dialog and focused the footer control. The live production DOM showed the same behavior. Close the settings dialog on legal-link navigation and move focus to the destination heading. [ConsentBanner.tsx](repo/src/components/consent/ConsentBanner.tsx:118) · [Live baseline](https://www.typhoon.band/de)
