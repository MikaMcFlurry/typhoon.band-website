## Reproduced and limitations

- **Browser matrix.** Chromium, Firefox and WebKit at 320 × 640, 390 × 844, 768 × 900, 1280 × 800 and 1440 × 900. The German home had zero document horizontal overflow at the five sizes. The header Booking target measured 44 px and reached the first field. With the current empty dates, Shows follows Gallery and precedes Booking in both page and menu order. 320 px checks on the three localized homes, their nine legal routes and checked errors also had zero document horizontal overflow.
- **Booking.** The round-two fix diff leaves serialization intact. Against the production baseline, the endpoint, server validator, honeypot, timing field and allowed event values are unchanged. All three engines sent the same ten keys to the interceptor through the normal submit flow: `name`, `email`, `event_type`, `event_location`, `event_date`, `message`, `phone`, `hp_field`, `locale` and `started_at`. Populated and empty optional values, spaces, newlines and the numeric timestamp matched the contract. Empty submit raised five field errors, focused Name and attempted zero requests. A missing event type focused the first radio. Space and ArrowRight worked. The preview, 502 recovery, terminal fallback, success and status focus were exercised. Every attempt used Playwright `page.route("**/api/booking", …)` with a local response or abort. Zero booking requests reached the backend.
- **Consent and playback.** With no gate mounted, the site notice stayed hidden. A synthetic late gate marker and event opened it for an undecided visitor in all three engines. “Nur notwendige” persisted `external_media: false` and a second event left the notice closed. Footer settings opened, closed with Escape and restored focus. The banner source scans for the initial marker and listens for mount events. The gate renders embed children after stored permission or a one-time opt-in. The published audio uses site-managed Supabase Storage, including metadata preloads, and advanced 1.84–2.00 seconds after clicking Play over the sampled interval. No download or native audio controls appeared. Toolbar traffic is recorded above.
- **SEO.** Home pages returned 200 with the correct locale HTML language, production self-canonical, reciprocal DE/EN/TR and German x-default, Open Graph and a large Twitter card. All three JSON-LD graphs parsed as WebSite, WebPage, MusicGroup and FAQPage, with nine members, eight tracks, six FAQs and zero events. Legal pages had route-specific canonical, alternate and og:url values. Signed-out admin redirected to each localized login. Login settled with `noindex, nofollow, nocache`, no canonical, no hreflang and no og:url. The preview itself sent HTTP `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`. The sitemap, robots, UTF-8 `/llms.txt` and German manifest start URL were verified. Chromium axe reported zero automated WCAG A/AA violations on the three homes, German privacy and German 404.
- **Motion.** On a fresh normal-motion home load, both below-fold tapes and all three hero tapes reached `clip-path: inset(0px)` after scrolling in all three engines. In reduced motion the page stayed in its visible state. The client-navigation case above remained broken in all three engines.
- **Read-only local checks.** `git diff --check 6871de1 903b973`, TypeScript with `--noEmit --incremental false` and the repository’s `next lint --no-cache` passed.
- **NOT_RUN.** Live booking delivery and persistence were excluded because the preview shares production services. Authenticated admin, real embed-provider loading and upcoming/TBA show states were unavailable for a read-only run. The consent mount path used a synthetic marker and event. Actual sitemap regeneration after changing content, native mail-client rendering, physical iOS, screen readers, new Lighthouse runs and a new build were outside the checks performed. Browser builds were Playwright Chromium, Firefox and WebKit on Windows. Visual captures were inspected in memory.
