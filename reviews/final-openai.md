# Typhoon release review · OpenAI

**Verdict** — **BLOCK**  
**Independence** — FRESH  
**Subject** — Production baseline `6871de1`, frozen review head `b5fc9a3`, and Vercel preview of runtime commit `ce39acf`. The commit diff from `ce39acf` to `b5fc9a3` contains only the decision record. Reviewed 23 November 2026.

## Findings

1. **P2 · OPENAI-01 · Blocking · The new mobile header Booking button leaves the menu covering the form.** On [preview home](https://typhoon-band-website-g7y0jr85t-mikamcflurrys-projects.vercel.app/de) at 390 × 844, open the hamburger menu, then tap the orange **Booking** button beside the close icon. Chromium, Firefox and WebKit all changed the hash to `#booking` while `aria-expanded` stayed `true` and the body stayed scroll-locked. The Booking row *inside* the menu has its own close handler. Give the header button the same behavior or hide it while the menu is open. Verify the form is visible and keyboard focus lands at a useful point on the booking destination. Evidence in `src/components/site/Header.tsx:85`, `:185` and `:215`.

2. **P2 · OPENAI-02 · Blocking · The e-mail recovery link sends the previous draft after an edit.** At 390 × 844, fill the form, mock a 502, then change the location and message. In Chromium, the visible preview updated and the still-visible e-mail link retained the original values. It also encoded line breaks as `%0A`. A customer can see their correction in the preview and open an e-mail with the old details. Generate the recovery link from the current validated draft or invalidate it on edit until it can be regenerated. Preserve the submitted snapshot for the terminal fallback, normalize body line endings to CRLF before encoding and provide a copyable draft for clients that truncate long URIs. Evidence in `src/components/sections/BookingForm.tsx:99`, `:141–155` and `:398–405`. [RFC 6068 `5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6068.html#section-5)

3. **P2 · OPENAI-03 · Blocking · The new FAQ publishes commitments still open for owner confirmation.** All three locales explicitly offer weddings and give a usual 48-hour reply. The decision record flags both for sign-off. The existing private-party form option shows what visitors can enquire about, and the live page already shows a 48-hour expectation. The branch now repeats these as direct answers in FAQ, JSON-LD and `/llms.txt`. The pricing answer also specifies which factors mainly determine the fee without a confirmed rate basis. Ask for event details and say that availability and a quote will be confirmed individually. Publish a wedding offer, response window and pricing factors once confirmed, using the same approved source across all three outputs. Evidence in `docs/redesign/2026-11-ux-seo-conversion.md:77–78` and `src/i18n/dictionaries.ts:545–559`, `:822–836` and `:1099–1113`.

4. **P2 · OPENAI-04 · Blocking · New form controls miss the accessibility bar.** At 320 × 640 in Chromium and Firefox, the German booking privacy link measured 142 × 42 CSS px. The English link also measured 42 px high. The new event chips have a 44 px height, but the unselected outline is about 2.6:1 against the dark fill. The fill and surrounding panel are too close in tone to delineate the chip without that outline. Set a real 44 px minimum hit area for the link and give the chip outline at least 3:1 contrast in its default state. Verify selected, hover, error and keyboard-focused states too. Evidence in `src/components/sections/BookingForm.tsx:383`, `src/app/globals.css:516–525` and `DESIGN.md:297`. [WCAG non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)

5. **P2 · OPENAI-05 · Blocking · Localized 404s regain the homepage metadata.** Visit `/de/gibt-es-nicht`, `/en/does-not-exist` or `/tr/bulunamadi` on preview. The response was HTTP 404 with `noindex`. Raw HTML included the intended `404 · Typhoon`, which explains how a substring check can pass. After load and again 1.1 seconds later, the three engines showed their localized home title and home canonical in the live DOM. Chromium also showed home hreflang and Open Graph tags. This leaves the error response and final page identifying different resources. Put the clear-out at the matched route level or scope home metadata to the home route, then assert one 404 title and zero canonical/hreflang tags in both the response and settled DOM. Evidence in `src/app/[locale]/(site)/not-found.tsx:6`, `src/app/[locale]/(site)/[...rest]/page.tsx:4` and `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx:59–98`.

6. **P2 · OPENAI-06 · Preview release gate · Vercel injects an ungated toolbar.** On a fresh browser profile, the preview requested `vercel.live/_next-live/feedback/feedback.js` before any site consent. Firefox additionally requested `feedback.html` and `/login/validate`. I observed none of those requests on the current production baseline. This is a preview configuration finding. Disable the toolbar on the owner-review preview and confirm the production deployment setting before promotion. [Vercel Toolbar visibility](https://vercel.com/docs/vercel-toolbar/managing-toolbar)

7. **P2 · OPENAI-07 · Non-blocking · WebKit still clips the focused textarea behind the audio dock.** On the preview at 320 × 568 and 390 × 844, play the featured track, jump to booking, then Tab from Name to Message. The textarea was 136 px high. Roughly 45 px remained above the dock when focused, with the rest behind it or below the viewport. Chromium kept the textarea clear in the settled check. This is partial obscuration and does not by itself establish a WCAG 2.4.11 failure. Add focus positioning that accounts for both fixed edges and verify with the dock open on WebKit. Evidence in `src/app/globals.css:111` and `src/components/audio/PlayerDock.tsx:46`. [WCAG focus not obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html)

8. **P2 · OPENAI-08 · Non-blocking · The image sitemap has no refresh policy tied to content edits.** Preview served parseable XML with 12 pages, 33 image entries and 12 identical `lastmod` timestamps generated by `new Date()`. This timestamp behavior predates the branch. The new Supabase-derived images run through the full home loader, and the route adds neither a revalidation interval nor an invalidation hook for image edits. Admin invalidation targets locale homes. Give the sitemap a defined image refresh policy, use only the relevant loaders and omit `lastmod` until a significant-change timestamp exists. `/llms.txt` already has a one-hour revalidation interval. Evidence in `src/app/sitemap.ts:8–31` and `src/lib/admin/revalidate.ts:10–11`. [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap)

9. **P3 · OPENAI-09 · Non-blocking · Tighten SEO semantics and metadata inheritance.** The JSON-LD parses, but `primaryImageOfPage` is a bare URL for the social graphic. Schema.org expects an `ImageObject` and the visible primary image is the collage. The graph also claims booking support in German, English and Turkish. Localized forms establish interface languages, while the languages the booking contact can serve still need owner confirmation. The legal pages now have correct `og:url` and title, but retain the homepage Twitter title and, without an admin override, the homepage marketing description. Use the hero image object, publish only verified contact languages, and provide route-specific legal sharing metadata. Evidence in `src/lib/seo/structured-data.ts:69–75` and `:137–146`, and `src/components/legal/LegalPage.tsx:31–59`. [Schema.org primary image](https://schema.org/primaryImageOfPage) · [Schema.org available language](https://schema.org/availableLanguage)

10. **P3 · OPENAI-10 · Non-blocking · Polish English and Turkish before approval.** The hero facts and genre claims match the current line-up and owner positioning. The localized metadata and booking CTAs make no fixed price, set length or availability guarantee. The German empty-state heading is supported and avoids implying dates are already in planning. EN “Shows on request” reads like a bespoke set offering. “No upcoming dates” with a booking invitation is clearer. “The demos play right at the top” can imply autoplay, so use “Listen in the setlist above.” In TR, replace “Grup … Hechingen’de yaşıyor” with “Grubun merkezi Hechingen’de.” The existing phrase describes where the members live. “Nefesli çalgı grubuyla birlikte {count} müzisyen” is clearer than “Nefeslilerle {count} müzisyen.” Use one register across “dinle,” “davet edin,” and “gönder,” and replace “Acil durumlarda” with “Acele bir talebiniz varsa.” In the newly published English `/llms.txt`, replace “without losing their handwriting” with “without losing their signature sound.” Evidence in `src/i18n/dictionaries.ts:652–655`, `:807–837`, `:881–883` and `:1084–1114`, plus `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts:23–25`.

## Reproduced

- **PASS — Browser matrix.** Chromium, Firefox and WebKit at 320 × 640, 390 × 844, 768 × 900, 1280 × 800 and 1440 × 900. First visit had no site consent notice on the ungated home. The closed-menu header CTA was 44 px high and landed with the first form field in view at all five sizes. With the current empty dates, the nav and page both placed Shows after Gallery and before Booking. The mobile menu at 320, 390 and 768 closed on Escape and returned focus. The three localized homes and checked legal/error pages had zero document horizontal overflow at 320 px.
- **PASS — Booking and FAQ.** Empty submit produced five inline errors, focused Name and made zero requests. With Name and E-mail valid, a missing event type focused the first radio. Space selected it, and arrow navigation selected the next event type and updated the preview. The mocked payload contained `name`, `email`, `event_type`, `event_location`, `event_date`, `message`, `phone`, `hp_field`, `locale` and `started_at`, using the same field names and values as the frozen diff expects. Mocked fallback and 502 links used the approved address and preserved punctuation, Unicode and optional fields. Mocked success reset the form. Six FAQ items rendered and opened by keyboard. All booking requests were intercepted in the browser. The production booking backend received none.
- **PASS — Consent and audio.** Footer settings opened and returned focus on Escape. Source inspection confirmed the gate renders children only after consent or its one-time action, with both a DOM marker and a mount event for notice discovery. A synthetic late marker/event on preview opened the notice for an undecided visitor and did not reopen it after “necessary only.” Playback advanced more than one second in all three engines. The dock added 88 px scroll padding and exposed no download or native audio controls. The direct Supabase stream observed after a play click is the site’s audio storage. The toolbar and WebKit focus exceptions are recorded above.
- **PASS — SEO and static checks.** The three homes served 200, correct `lang`, production-origin self-canonical, reciprocal DE/EN/TR and German `x-default`, complete Open Graph and large-card Twitter metadata. All three JSON-LD graphs parsed and listed the visible line-up, eight demos, six FAQs and zero events. Preview's HTTP `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` applied independently. Legal canonicals and alternates were route-specific. `robots.txt` pointed to the production sitemap and excluded the API and localized admin paths. The manifest launched at `/de`. `/llms.txt` served UTF-8 text. Chromium axe reported zero automatic WCAG A/AA violations on the three homes, German privacy and German 404. Manual checks found the issues above. `git diff --check` passed on the frozen range. Checkout `tsc --noEmit --incremental false` passed. A concurrent modification to `MotionInit.tsx` appeared during review, so that type check does not certify a clean frozen build.
- **NOT_RUN.** Live booking persistence and delivery were intentionally excluded because the preview shares production infrastructure. No live gate instance or upcoming/TBA show existed on the preview, so those branches were source-reviewed and the consent notice event was tested synthetically. No physical iOS device, screen reader, native mail client, sitemap refresh after a real content edit, or independent Lighthouse median was run. The review used browser and source checks without running a build that writes to the checkout.

## Design sign-off

Withhold sign-off pending the booking, copy and accessibility fixes. The 390 px comparison (`pairs/01-mobile-first-visit-390.png`) shows a clearer entry into both primary actions, with the gold signature, square tape and uncropped collage intact. Direct contact before the form and the shorter empty preview make the booking section easier to read. The menu comparison (`pairs/22-menu-390.png`) shows two orange Booking controls in one open state, and the 390 px FAQ (`pairs/12-faq-390.png`) puts the first answer below a title, intro, repeat CTA and phone block. Tighten that stack so the answer is visible sooner. At 320 × 640 my Chromium capture placed Play at `y=596–644`, the hero Booking button at `y=656–704` and the poster at `y=821`. The poster starts about 60 px lower than on production with consent dismissed. Keep the header booking access and tighten this short-phone hero before visual approval. At 1280 × 800 and 1440 × 900 the final playable setlist row is visible, while the footer of the paper sheet falls 41–54 px below the first fold on both production and preview. The legal comparison (`pairs/17-imprint-320.png`) shows a material improvement in how the long German imprint heading wraps.
